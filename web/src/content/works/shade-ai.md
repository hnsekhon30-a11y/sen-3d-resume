## What I built

ShadeAI is a personal AI makeup and color-analysis assistant designed to help users understand their skin tone, undertone and compatible makeup shades across brands.

## Core idea

Analyze visual and questionnaire signals, map them to a structured color profile, then recommend compatible products and shades instead of making users manually compare dozens of swatches.

## Product direction

- Facial and color-feature analysis
- Undertone and depth classification
- Cross-brand shade matching
- Product recommendation experience
- Structured shade dataset with HEX / CIELAB / undertone metadata

## My focus

Product thinking, data structure, AI-assisted recommendations and turning a messy shopping decision into a guided experience.
