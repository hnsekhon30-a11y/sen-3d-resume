## Problem

Sales and marketing teams can spend significant time researching leads before deciding who deserves attention.

## Solution

A workflow concept that combines lead enrichment, firmographic signals, scoring logic and AI-generated context to prioritize accounts and make the next action clearer.

## Focus areas

- Lead enrichment
- Predictive scoring
- AI summaries
- Account prioritization
- Workflow automation

The goal is not just a score, but an explainable recommendation that can feed downstream sales workflows.
