## Goal

Build a dashboard to understand how AI-driven traffic is reaching a website and turn that traffic into measurable business insight.

## Work

- GA4 data analysis
- AI traffic segmentation
- KPI design
- Looker Studio dashboarding
- Key-event and conversion tracking review

## Outcome

A reporting framework that makes AI-originated traffic visible and creates a foundation for measuring engagement and conversion behavior.
