# AI Makeup Shade Finder

**AI beauty & color analysis**

## Overview
An AI product concept designed to help users identify suitable makeup shades across brands using facial/color attributes and structured product shade data.

## Focus
- Skin tone and undertone analysis
- Shade depth classification
- Cross-brand shade matching
- Product recommendations
- Structured shade metadata

## Product direction
The experience is designed around reducing the friction of manually comparing foundation and makeup shades across multiple brands.

## Stack
AI · Computer Vision · Recommendation Systems · Structured Color Data
