## Goal

Explore a lightweight operations system for a forging business covering inventory, production and dispatch.

## Approach

Use AppSheet with Google Sheets as a practical low-code layer for capturing operational data and making production information easier to access.

## Areas

- Inventory
- Production status
- Dispatch tracking
- Operational visibility
- Data capture

The project focuses on translating real operational workflows into a maintainable digital process.
