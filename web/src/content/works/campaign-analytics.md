## Focus

A relational campaign analytics dataset used to answer business questions around spend, conversions, ROI, CPM and campaign duration.

## Work

- SQL joins across campaign tables
- ROI calculations
- Conversion-rate analysis
- CPM analysis
- Duration metrics
- Correlation and pivot-style analysis

## Takeaway

The project demonstrates how raw campaign tables can be turned into decision-ready marketing metrics with SQL.
